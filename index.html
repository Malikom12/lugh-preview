Appointments

Phone 042 932 7466 to book. If it’s urgent, tell us when you call.

Book by phone

Our phone lines are open during surgery hours, Monday to Friday. Mornings are busiest, so if your call isn’t urgent, try after 11am.

Getting the most from your appointment

  • Bring a list of the medicines you take, or bring the boxes.
  • If you have more than one thing to discuss, tell us when you book so we can give you enough time.
  • Need help with English? Tell us when you book.
  • If you can’t come, please phone to cancel so someone else can have your appointment.

Test results

Most blood test results are back within a week. If Dr Mishra needs to see you about a result, we’ll contact you. Otherwise, phone us in the afternoon for your result.

Home visits

If you’re too unwell to come to the surgery, phone before 10.30am. Dr Mishra will decide whether a home visit is needed.

Certificates, forms and reports

Ask at reception for medical certificates, insurance forms and reports. Some of these have a fee, listed on our fees page.

Prescriptions

Order the medicines on your repeat list without an appointment. We’ll send the prescription to your pharmacy.

How repeat prescriptions work

  1. Ask for your repeat medicines

    Phone us, or use the request form below.

  2. Dr Mishra checks your request

    She’ll review your medicines and let us know if she needs to see you first.

  3. We send it to your pharmacy

    Prescriptions go by Healthmail, the HSE’s secure email for doctors and pharmacies, so there’s nothing to collect.

  4. Your pharmacy gets it ready

    Check with your pharmacy before you call in.

Please allow 2 working days. Order a week before you run out, especially before public holidays.

When you’ll need an appointment

  • A new medicine, or a change to the dose of one you take.
  • Your medication review. We’ll ask to see you at least once a year if you take regular medicines.

Free contraception

If you’re aged 17 to 35, your contraception appointments and prescriptions are free under the HSE Free Contraception Scheme. You don’t need a medical card.

Register as a new patient

Here’s how to join the practice, and what to bring with you.

How to register

  1. Phone us first

    Our list is sometimes full. Phone 042 932 7466 and we’ll tell you straight away if we can take you.

  2. Fill in a registration form

    Collect one at reception.

  3. Bring your documents

    See the list below.

  4. We’ll ask for your records

    With your permission, we’ll ask your previous GP to send your medical records to us.

What to bring

  • Photo ID, such as a passport or driving licence
  • Your PPS number
  • Your medical card or GP visit card, if you have one
  • A list of the medicines you take, or the boxes
  • Your previous GP’s name and address

If you have a medical card or GP visit card

To make us your GP under your card, you’ll need to change your doctor with the HSE. We’ll help you with the form.

Children under 8 can see a GP for free with the under-8s GP visit card. Register them at hse.ie (opens in a new tab).

Students

Studying at DkIT and living in Dundalk? You can register with us while you’re here. If you’re 17 to 35, contraception is free.

When we’re closed

Where to get care in the evening, at weekends and on public holidays. Most urgent first.

  1. Life-threatening emergency

    Call 112 or 999

    For example chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, signs of a stroke, heavy bleeding, or someone who is unconscious.

    The nearest emergency department is at Our Lady of Lourdes Hospital, Drogheda.

  2. Weekday evenings and nights

    042 932 7466

    Phone the practice. Our recorded message gives you the number for the Dundalk GP on call that night.

  3. Weekends and public holidays

    086 813 5511

    Phone Dealgan Doc, the Dundalk GP out-of-hours service.

  4. Minor injuries, age 5 and over

    Dundalk Injury Unit

    Louth County Hospital, Dublin Road, Dundalk A91 X0DP. Open 9am to 8pm every day. Go here for recent injuries such as sprains, cuts, minor burns or a possible broken bone in an arm or leg.

  5. Medicines and minor illness

    Pharmacists can advise on minor illnesses and your medicines, without an appointment.

  6. If you’re struggling to cope

    Samaritans: phone 116 123, free, any time. Or text 50808 for free, any time.

  7. Questions about health services

    HSE Live: 1800 700 700.

Privacy

How this website handles information.

Who runs this website

This is a design concept by Muhammad Malik. It isn’t run by Lugh Medical Centre.

What this website collects

Nothing. There are no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and nothing is loaded from other websites.

The free GP care checker works entirely in your browser. The repeat prescription form is a demonstration, so nothing you type is sent or saved.

If you change the text size, your choice is saved in your browser so the site remembers it. It isn’t sent anywhere.

On a live practice website

Prescription requests would be used only to deal with the request. Health information is a special category of data under the GDPR, so it would be processed to provide healthcare under Article 9(2)(h), and only the practice team would see it. Patients could ask to see or correct their information, or complain to the Data Protection Commission (opens in a new tab).

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard or magnification.

What we’ve done

  • Text size buttons at the top of every page.
  • The Atkinson Hyperlegible Next typeface, designed by the Braille Institute for readers with low vision. It keeps similar-looking characters apart, so phone numbers and Eircodes are easier to read.
  • Strong colour contrast, aiming for WCAG 2.2 level AA throughout.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a clear outline showing where you are.
  • No time limits or auto-playing media. Motion is reduced if your device asks for it.
  • Phone numbers you can tap to call.

How it’s been checked

Every page passes an automated WCAG 2.2 AA check with axe-core, on phone and desktop screens, by day and by night.

Known gaps

  • Translations cover the key information only.
  • It hasn’t been tested with screen-reader users yet.
